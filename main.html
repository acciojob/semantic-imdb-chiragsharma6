Summary

Interstellar follows Cooper, a former NASA pilot who travels through a wormhole with a team of astronauts to find a new habitable planet for humanity. The journey explores space, time, sacrifice, and the bond between a father and his daughter.

Actors

Matthew McConaughey plays Cooper, Anne Hathaway plays Dr. Amelia Brand, Jessica Chastain plays Murph, and Michael Caine plays Professor Brand.
